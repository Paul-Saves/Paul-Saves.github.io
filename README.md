# Academic Portfolio — Dr. Paul Saves ([paul-saves.github.io](https://paul-saves.github.io/))

Welcome to the source repository for my professional academic portfolio, hosted at [paul-saves.github.io](https://paul-saves.github.io/).

This site serves as a comprehensive hub for my research in **Frugal & Explainable AI (XAI)**, **Surrogate Modeling**, and **Decision-Making under Uncertainty for Complex Socio-Environmental Systems**, as a Tenured Research Scientist (Chargé de Recherche) at the French National Research Institute for Sustainable Development (**IRD** — UMI 209 UMMISCO / Sorbonne Université) and Associate Researcher at **IRIT / CNRS**.

## 🛠️ Technical Structure

- `_data/navigation.yml`: Centralized control for the site's header navigation.
- `_layouts/`: Custom HTML templates (`default`, `page`, `post`) providing a consistent academic layout.
- `_pages/`:
  - `research.md`: Research framework, directions, strategic projects, and supervision history.
  - `publications.md`: Complete bibliography with direct DOI, HAL, and arXiv links.
  - `software.md`: Open-source toolboxes and solvers (SMT 2.0, SEGOMOE, benchmark platforms).
  - `teaching.md`: Pedagogical statement, core curriculum, and course portfolio.
  - `team.md`: Supervision, student mentoring, and collaborative network.
  - `contact.md`: Professional affiliations, office locations, and contact details.
- `assets/`: Academic documents (CV, dossiers), publication PDFs, and media assets.
- `css/main.css`: Professional typography and layout styling.
- `index.md`: Biography, career summary, milestones, and awards.
